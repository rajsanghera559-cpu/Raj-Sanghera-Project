# Layer 1 — Mathematical Language

The goal is not memorization.

The goal is:

> recognize the small set of concepts that appear repeatedly.

Across CFD / turbulence / numerical simulation, a surprisingly small vocabulary dominates.

---

# 1. Function

### Frequency

★★★★★ (appears everywhere)

### Definition

A rule that maps:

[
x \rightarrow f(x)
]

Input → output.

Examples:

[
f(x)=x^2
]

Input:

[
2
]

Output:

[
4
]

Fluid example:

[
u(x,y,t)
]

velocity depends on:

* x position
* y position
* time

---

# 2. Field

### Frequency

★★★★★

### Definition

A quantity assigned everywhere in space.

Examples:

Scalar field:

[
T(x,y)
]

temperature.

Vector field:

[
u(x,y)
]

velocity.

In your simulation:

[
\omega(x,y)
]

vorticity field.

Visual:

```text
→ → →
↗ ↑ ↖
← ← ←
```

Each point stores information.

---

# 3. Vector

### Frequency

★★★★★

### Definition

Magnitude + direction.

Notation:

[
\mathbf{u}=(u,v)
]

Example:

velocity.

Magnitude:

[
|\mathbf{u}|=\sqrt{u^2+v^2}
]

---

# 4. Derivative

### Frequency

★★★★★

### Definition

Rate of change.

[
\frac{df}{dx}
]

Meaning:

> how quickly something changes.

Example:

Position:

[
x(t)
]

Velocity:

[
v=\frac{dx}{dt}
]

Acceleration:

[
a=\frac{d^2x}{dt^2}
]

---

# 5. Gradient

### Frequency

★★★★★

### Definition

Direction of fastest increase.

Symbol:

[
\nabla f
]

Example:

[
\nabla T
]

temperature increases fastest here.

Visual:

```text
cold → warm → hot
```

Pressure force:

[
-\nabla p
]

---

# 6. Divergence

### Frequency

★★★★★

### Definition

Measures expansion/compression.

Symbol:

[
\nabla\cdot u
]

Interpretation:

Positive:

```text
↖ ↑ ↗
← • →
↙ ↓ ↘
```

Negative:

everything converges inward.

In incompressible flow:

[
\nabla\cdot u=0
]

No volume creation.

---

# 7. Curl

### Frequency

★★★★★

### Definition

Measures rotation.

Symbol:

[
\nabla\times u
]

2D:

[
\omega=
\frac{\partial v}{\partial x}
-----------------------------

\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}
]

This is YOUR project.

Visual:

```text
↺
```

---

# 8. Laplacian

### Frequency

★★★★★

### Definition

Measures local spreading/smoothing.

Symbol:

[
\nabla^2
]

Formula:

[
\nabla^2f
=========

\frac{\partial^2f}{\partial x^2}
+
\frac{\partial^2f}{\partial y^2}
]

In your solver:

[
\nu\nabla^2\omega
]

Diffusion.

---

# 9. Fourier Transform

### Frequency

★★★★★

### Definition

Convert:

Space → frequencies

[
f(x)
\rightarrow
F(k)
]

Interpretation:

Every pattern becomes waves.

Visual:

```text
signal
↓

frequency components
```

Your spectrum:

[
E(k)
]

energy by scale.

---

# 10. Eigenvalue

### Frequency

★★★★☆

### Definition

Special scaling direction.

[
Av=\lambda v
]

Interpretation:

transform changes size but not direction.

Applications:

* stability
* turbulence
* PCA
* quantum mechanics

---

# 11. Partial Differential Equation (PDE)

### Frequency

★★★★★

### Definition

Equation involving change in multiple variables.

Example:

[
\frac{\partial u}{\partial t}
=============================

\nu\nabla^2u
]

Heat equation.

Navier–Stokes:

[
\frac{\partial u}{\partial t}
+
(u\cdot\nabla)u
===============

-\nabla p
+
\nu\nabla^2u
]

---

# 12. Convolution

### Frequency

★★★★☆

### Definition

Interaction across neighborhoods.

Symbol:

[
f*g
]

Spectral insight:

Convolution in space

↓

Multiplication in Fourier space.
